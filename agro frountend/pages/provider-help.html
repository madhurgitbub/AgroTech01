📋 Provider FAQs & Best Practices

1. How do I add and manage my farm equipment?
▼
Go to "Add Service" in your navigation bar, fill in the details (equipment name, pricing, unit, photo, location) and click Publish. You can manage, edit or deactivate listings anytime from "My Services".
2. How do I get notified of new customer requests?
▼
Whenever a farmer books your service, it immediately appears in your "Requests" tab and an alert is added to your "Alerts" center.
3. What happens when I accept a request?
▼
Accepting a request sends an instant confirmation alert to the farmer with your contact details so you both can coordinate delivery or service execution.
4. How do I temporarily pause an equipment listing? / लिस्टिंग को अस्थायी रूप से कैसे रोकें?
▼
Go to "My Services" and use the toggle switch next to any service card. Turning it off hides the equipment from search results while preserving all details for when you want to reactivate it.
