📋 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I request equipment or farm services? / मशीनरी या सेवा कैसे बुक करें?
▼
Go to "Find Services", browse machinery (tractors, harvesters, rotavators, etc.) or seeds/fertilizers, click "Request Service", fill in your required acres/quantity and date, and click submit. The provider will receive an instant alert to accept your request.
2. How do payments work? / भुगतान प्रक्रिया कैसे काम करती है?
▼
AgroTech supports Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as UPI, online cards, and wallet payments. You can pay securely upon completion of the service or as agreed with the provider.
3. How do I track my request status? / अनुरोध की स्थिति कैसे देखें?
▼
Visit the "My Requests" tab in your navigation bar to view all pending, accepted, and completed requests in real time. You also receive alerts in your "Alerts" center whenever a provider accepts or updates your request.
4. Can I cancel a service request? / क्या अनुरोध रद्द किया जा सकता है?
▼
Yes, if the provider has not yet started the work or dispatched machinery, you can contact the provider directly using their phone number shown on the request card.
5. How do I switch language to Hindi? / भाषा हिंदी में कैसे बदलें?
▼
Click the language toggle button (🇬🇧 EN / 🇮🇳 हिंदी) in the top navbar at any time. The entire application updates smoothly.
