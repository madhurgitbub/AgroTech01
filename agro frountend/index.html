Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about machinery booking, payments, fuel policy, and operator dispatch.

How is the per-acre machinery rental cost calculated? ▾
Pricing is standardized based on equipment horsepower, implements used, and exact acreage. When you book on AgroTECH, the total price is calculated upfront with zero hidden broker fees. You only pay for the verified acreage worked on your field.
Is diesel/fuel and driver included with the machinery? ▾
Yes! For heavy machinery like tractors and combine harvesters, the listing description explicitly states operator and diesel inclusion. The provider sends an experienced operator along with the machinery to ensure precision field work.
What happens if weather interrupts the work or rain occurs? ▾
AgroTECH provides a flexible rescheduling policy. If sudden rain or adverse weather halts field operations, you and the provider can reschedule the remaining acreage to the next suitable sunny date without cancellation penalties.
What if a machine breaks down during field operation? ▾
In the rare event of mechanical failure, our local service network dispatches a replacement tractor or harvester within the same cluster to ensure your sowing or harvesting schedule is never delayed.
How and when do I make payment for the service? ▾
AgroTECH follows a farmer-first policy: you inspect the completed work on your field first, and then pay the confirmed amount directly to the service provider via UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, or Cash upon job completion.
